## Why it stands out

Sony's fifth-generation flagship keeps the class-leading ANC while trimming weight and refining the earcup seal. Call quality is noticeably improved for remote work.

## Who should buy

- Frequent flyers and commuters who value quiet over everything else
- Anyone who wears headphones 4+ hours a day and needs comfort first

## Caveats

The touch controls take a day to get used to, and the case is bulkier than some competitors. For pure portability, in-ear models may fit better in a small bag.
